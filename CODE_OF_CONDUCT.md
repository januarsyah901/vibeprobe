# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short adaptation; the full text there applies where this one is silent.

## Our pledge

We want participation in vibeprobe to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Newcomers and people who are learning security are especially welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming bad intent.
- Keep security discussions responsible: no exploit details for unpatched vulnerabilities in public threads (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory jokes.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using vibeprobe, or this project's channels, to coordinate attacks on systems you do not own.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they deem harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (**Security → Report a vulnerability**, mention "conduct"), or contact the maintainer [@januarsyah901](https://github.com/januarsyah901) through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
